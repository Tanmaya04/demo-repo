How to get started

  1. Start your Project

    Discover our manufacturing capabilities, previous projects, and FAQs to learn about the manufacturing process and full range of product options.

  2. Register on LabwearOS

    Sign up for free – it only takes 2 minutes! This gives you access to our platform where you can explore features, manage orders, and track your project.

  3. Configure your Tech Pack in LabwearOS

    Select your custom measurements, fabrics, colors, labels, and use Illustrator for embellishment, including reference images for finishings. Before uploading, double-check for accuracy and completeness.

  4. Submit your Design(s)

    Once your Digital Tech Pack is complete, submit your Design through LabwearOS to receive quotation and initiate production.

  5. Access Assistance

    Our experts support you in optimizing your project, finding solutions across our network of factories, and product development. Use the built-in chat or book a call any time.

  6. Sampling

    Before production starts, you can request either 3D sample or physical samples for final approval of materials, construction, fit, color, or embellishment.

  7. Bulk Production

    Once the sample is approved, bulk production starts. You can track the progress in real-time and stay updated at every stage of the process.

  8. Quality Control & Shipping

    Each garment gets quality checked by the manufacturer according to our guidelines and sent directly to you.
